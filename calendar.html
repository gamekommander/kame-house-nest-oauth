Personal Calendar Integration

A private integration for accessing and maintaining calendar information in an account authorized by its owner.

Purpose

The integration uses Google Calendar APIs, with the account owner's permission, to read calendar events and, where authorized, create or update calendar events. Its purpose is private calendar synchronization and personal scheduling. It is not a public service and does not offer sign-up for other users.

Authorization

Access requires Google OAuth consent from the account owner. The owner can revoke access in Google Account settings.

Privacy

This public website provides information only. It does not access calendars, collect event information, or process OAuth callbacks. See the privacy policy for details about the private integration.
