Privacy policy

Calendar data stays private.

Effective October 18, 2026.

Scope and operator

This policy covers a private, personal-use Google Calendar integration operated by the owner of the GitHub account gamekommander, as well as this public informational website. The integration is not offered to other users.

Information accessed and why

With the account owner's authorization, the integration may access calendar lists, calendar identifiers, event titles, descriptions, dates, times, locations, and other event metadata available through the granted Google Calendar permissions. Where the owner grants write permissions, the integration may create, modify, or remove calendar events as part of private synchronization and scheduling workflows. OAuth tokens are used to maintain authorized access.

Storage and retention

Calendar information and authorization credentials are processed within the owner's private systems and through Google services as necessary for synchronization. Local configuration, logs, calendar records, and backups may retain data under the owner's settings. No fixed retention period or immediate backup deletion is promised.

This public website does not receive calendar contents or OAuth credentials, and its public repository must not contain private calendar records, access tokens, or client secrets.

Sharing and use limitations

Calendar data is used for the owner's personal synchronization and scheduling. It may be exchanged with Google to carry out authorized calendar operations. It is not sold, used for advertising, transferred to data brokers, or used to train generalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Website hosting

This static website is hosted by GitHub Pages and does not add analytics, tracking pixels, cookies, forms, or browser storage. GitHub may process visitor information to operate and secure hosting under its privacy statement.

Revoking access and deletion

The owner may revoke the integration's access through Google Account connections. Revoking access prevents future authorized API access but does not automatically erase data already retained in local records, logs, or backups. Such copies must be removed separately where applicable.

Questions and changes

For non-sensitive questions, contact the maintainer through the repository's public issues page. Do not post personal calendar details or credentials. This policy may be updated if data practices change.
